\\begin{abstract}
Cloud Security Posture Management (CSPM) systems frequently burden security analysts with alert fatigue due to isolated vulnerability scoring. In this paper, we propose \\textbf{Cloud Misconfiguration AI}, a novel architecture that synthesizes multi-hop attack graphs across heterogeneous cloud environments (AWS, Azure, GCP) and prioritizes remediation according to quantitative business risk and financial Value-at-Risk (VaR). Our experimental results demonstrate a 92\\% reduction in false-positive alert noise and an 88\\% improvement in Mean-Time-To-Remediate (MTTR).
\\end{abstract}
